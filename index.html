What is a reef?

A City Built by Tiny Builders

Coral reefs are formed by colonies of tiny animals called polyps that secrete calcium carbonate skeletons. Over centuries, millions of these skeletons stack into the largest living structures on Earth.

🐠

Biodiversity Hotspot

Home to 4,000+ fish species and countless invertebrates, corals, and algae.

🌊

Natural Barrier

Reefs absorb up to 97% of wave energy, shielding coastlines from storms.

🧪

Medicine Cabinet

Reef organisms are used in treatments for cancer, heart disease, and pain.

🌍

Climate Regulator

Reefs cycle nutrients and support the ocean's carbon and nitrogen balance.

The crisis

Reefs Under Pressure

Half of the world's coral reefs have already been lost. Without action, up to 90% could vanish by 2050.

🌡️

Climate Change & Bleaching

50%+

Rising temperatures force corals to expel their algae, turning them white and starving them.

🏭

Pollution & Runoff

40%

Agricultural runoff and plastic smother reefs and block the sunlight corals need.

⚓

Overfishing & Damage

30%

Destructive fishing and physical damage break fragile coral structures.

🧊

Ocean Acidification

25%

Absorbed CO₂ makes the ocean more acidic, dissolving coral skeletons.
